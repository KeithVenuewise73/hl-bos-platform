# Launch checklist — Orchard Park Pizza

Demo version 1, draft. Prepared 2026-10-14.
Nothing on this list has been done. Domain, hosting and anything paid are decisions, not steps.

- [ ] **Content** — Walk Orchard Park Pizza's owner through the demo; replace every Sample item with their real services, story and photos, and get their written approval of the final wording.
  - Why: 5 item(s) on the demo are marked Sample.
  - Owner: Business owner
- [ ] **Domain** — Choose a domain with the owner and register it in the business's own name and account. HLG never registers a prospect's name.
  - Why: No website of their own was found.
  - Owner: Business owner
- [ ] **Hosting** — Decide where the site is hosted and on what terms (HLG-managed hosting or the owner's own).
  - Why: Hosting is a paid service and an ongoing commitment.
  - Owner: CEO decision
- [ ] **Google profile** — Add the new website to the Google Business Profile (owner signs in or grants HLG manager access).
  - Why: The Google listing shows no website.
  - Owner: Business owner
- [ ] **Google profile** — Add opening hours to the Google Business Profile.
  - Why: The Google listing shows no hours.
  - Owner: Business owner
- [ ] **Google profile** — Add the owner's own photos to the Google Business Profile and the site.
  - Why: The Google listing has no photos.
  - Owner: Business owner
- [ ] **Calls and texts** — Confirm (716) 555-0170 is the right number for calls and can receive text messages before the Call and Text buttons go live.
  - Why: Calls and texts are the main way local customers get in touch.
  - Owner: Business owner
- [ ] **Content** — Get the real menu (with prices, if the owner wants them shown) and replace the sample menu section.
  - Why: Customers look for the menu first.
  - Owner: Business owner
- [ ] **Ordering** — Decide with the owner whether to offer online ordering (for example Toast or Square Online) and link it.
  - Why: Ordering ahead is a common reason to visit a restaurant site.
  - Owner: Business owner
- [ ] **Leads and CRM** — Decide where contact-form messages go (the owner's email, or a CRM) before the form is switched on.
  - Why: The demo form is switched off until this is decided.
  - Owner: CEO decision
- [ ] **Reviews** — If the owner wants more reviews, set up an honest review-request process: ask every customer, never only the happy ones, and never write or buy reviews.
  - Why: Google shows 3.6 from 240 review(s) (Google Maps).
  - Owner: HLG
- [ ] **Measurement** — Add privacy-respecting visit and call tracking so the owner can see what the site does for them.
  - Why: Results should be measured, not assumed.
  - Owner: HLG
