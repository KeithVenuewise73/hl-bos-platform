# Launch checklist — Union Road Barbers

Demo version 1, draft. Prepared 2026-10-14.
Nothing on this list has been done. Domain, hosting and anything paid are decisions, not steps.

- [ ] **Content** — Walk Union Road Barbers's owner through the demo; replace every Sample item with their real services, story and photos, and get their written approval of the final wording.
  - Why: 5 item(s) on the demo are marked Sample.
  - Owner: Business owner
- [ ] **Domain** — Keep their existing domain (unionbarbers.example); with the owner's login, point it at the new site.
  - Why: They already have a website address; changing it would lose what people know.
  - Owner: Business owner
- [ ] **Hosting** — Decide where the site is hosted and on what terms (HLG-managed hosting or the owner's own).
  - Why: Hosting is a paid service and an ongoing commitment.
  - Owner: CEO decision
- [ ] **Calls and texts** — Confirm (716) 555-0150 is the right number for calls and can receive text messages before the Call and Text buttons go live.
  - Why: Their current site has no click-to-call.
  - Owner: Business owner
- [ ] **Booking** — Connect the Book button to the booking tool they already use (Vagaro).
  - Why: Booking is the main action for a salon or barber.
  - Owner: Business owner
- [ ] **Leads and CRM** — Decide where contact-form messages go (the owner's email, or a CRM) before the form is switched on.
  - Why: Their current site has no contact or quote form.
  - Owner: CEO decision
- [ ] **Reviews** — If the owner wants more reviews, set up an honest review-request process: ask every customer, never only the happy ones, and never write or buy reviews.
  - Why: No Google rating was available.
  - Owner: HLG
- [ ] **Measurement** — Add privacy-respecting visit and call tracking so the owner can see what the site does for them.
  - Why: No analytics were found on their current site.
