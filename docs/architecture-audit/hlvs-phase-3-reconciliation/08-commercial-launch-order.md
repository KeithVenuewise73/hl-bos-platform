# 08 · Recommended Commercial Launch Order

The order to bring Herman Legacy products to market, chosen for **time-to-revenue ÷ effort**, using reuse before rebuild. Products are ranked by how close they are to sellable, how much they lean on the already-built spine, and their strategic/revenue weight (from report 04).

## The launch sequence

### 1 — HL-BTI (Business Transformation Intelligence) — **launch first**

The only product that is genuinely near-complete (80%), fully assembled on the spine, with a clear buyer (HSCS Consulting) and high-value engagements. Remaining: deploy + first live customer + pricing. **This is the fastest real revenue.**

### 2 — VisibilityAI — **launch second (and it feeds everything else)**

The discovery→assessment→proposal front door. It is the **lead-generation engine for the whole portfolio** — every other product's sales motion starts with a VisibilityAI assessment. 40% done but ~80% reusable; remaining is UI + the scan worker over the existing engine. High strategic leverage.

### 3 — Managed services & the "available" service menu — **sell now, no new build**

14 of the 25 catalogued services are already deliverable (website creation/modernization, local visibility, SEO, review management, communications, hosting/support, managed services, dashboards, CRM setup, lead capture, payments, appointment scheduling, business discovery). These are **revenue available today** once pricing is set — many are human-delivered or thin wrappers over live modules. Bundle them as the services arm while software products mature.

### 4 — SalonAI — **first assembled vertical**

The proof of the Factory. High reuse (~85%); a defined vertical template exists. Launching it demonstrates the assembly line and creates a repeatable pattern for the rest of the vertical catalog.

### 5 — Review Management / Reputation Recovery — **quick vertical wins**

Built on `visibility.reviews` (~80% reusable); modest remaining effort; clear standalone value and natural upsell from a VisibilityAI assessment.

### 6 — HomeHuddle & the Huddle community suite — **after the Venuewise decision**

Real demand and live legacy proof, but blocked on the **convergence-vs-coexistence** decision. Do not launch on HL-BOS until that is settled, or you build the community domain twice. High reuse once unblocked.

### 7 — ReceptionAI / TransportationAI — **on-demand verticals**

Need a net-new engine each (AI receptionist, route assessment) but reuse the spine (~70–75%). Launch when a named customer justifies the engine build.

### 8 — HighlightAI — **the frontier bet (not a near-term launch)**

Genuinely greenfield; real, large investment with no reuse dividend. Launch only after Waves 1–3 fund it and a manual verification confirms nothing exists to reuse. Potentially high media revenue, but it is a new capability, not a catalog fill.

### 9 — BroadcastAI — **only after HighlightAI proves the media stack.**

### Not on the launch path (out of scope / decisions)

- **Legacy products** (HLVS Venture Studio, HSCS-GLP, RecoveryWise) — live but unreachable; any commercialization needs a separate approved plan (and RecoveryWise carries an unremediated security finding).
- **CoachAI, FleetHuddle, LandscapeAI** — planned concepts; assemble on demand.

## Cross-cutting prerequisites (gate every launch)

Nothing ships until these are done once:

1. **Pricing, licensing, module ownership set** (CEO) — no product sells without them.
2. **Stripe adapter live** — no product charges without it.
3. **Runtime switched on** — no product runs without it.

## One-paragraph recommendation

**Lead with HL-BTI for immediate revenue and VisibilityAI as the funnel; sell the already-deliverable services menu in parallel for cash flow now; prove the Factory with SalonAI; resolve Venuewise before touching the Huddles; and treat HighlightAI/BroadcastAI as a separately-funded frontier, not a catalog item.** This order turns the large, already-built foundation into revenue in the shortest path, spends reuse before rebuild at every step, and defers the only genuinely expensive (greenfield) work until the platform is paying for it.
