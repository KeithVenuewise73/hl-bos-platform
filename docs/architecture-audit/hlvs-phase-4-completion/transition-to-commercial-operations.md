# Recommendation: Transition from Platform Development to Commercial Operations

## The recommendation

**Herman Legacy should transition from platform development to commercial operations now** — conditionally. The engineering platform is complete enough to produce commercial software from reusable assets: the Software Factory can assemble every product from registered modules, proven end-to-end. The transition is gated not by more engineering, but by a small set of **CEO decisions and access grants**. Make those, and the business is in production.

## Why now

| Signal                                               | Reading                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Platform completion **92%**                          | The reusable spine is built and tested; only runtime deployment remains                 |
| Factory completion **70%**                           | The assembly machinery works; the missing 30% is ignition + decisions, not construction |
| **8/8 products assemblable** from registered modules | No product needs a new foundation — the reuse dividend is realized                      |
| **1 product ready to launch** (HL-BTI)               | There is immediate revenue to capture                                                   |
| Commercial readiness **0%**                          | The _only_ thing missing to sell is your commercial terms                               |

The pattern is unambiguous: **the engineering risk is retired; the remaining risk is commercial and operational.** That is precisely the point at which a company shifts from building the factory to running it.

## The conditions (what "conditionally" means)

The transition completes when these are supplied — none is engineering:

1. **Commercial terms** — pricing, licensing, module ownership (report `remaining-critical-tasks.md` #1). Without them, nothing can be sold, regardless of how ready the software is.
2. **Runtime ignition** — grant the Anthropic key and authorize deploying the built edge runtime (#2). Without it, products are inert.
3. **Payment rails** — the Stripe adapter (#3). Without it, nothing can be charged.

Everything else (reporting, agent wiring, the module-registry seed, further verticals) can proceed _in parallel with_ commercial operations, not before them.

## Recommended operating model after transition

- **Run the Factory, don't rebuild it.** New products are _assembled_ from registered modules via the compositions defined in Phase IV — not hand-written. The assembler already verifies this is possible before any build starts.
- **Lead with HL-BTI + VisibilityAI**, sell the already-deliverable services menu for cash flow, and prove the assembly line with SalonAI (Phase III launch order).
- **Keep the guardrails absolute** — the "no second X" rule, the duplicate-risk check, and the register-before-you-build governance are what keep the estate clean as it scales commercially.
- **Treat the greenfield frontier (HighlightAI/BroadcastAI) as a separately-funded venture**, not a platform-completion item.

## What the CEO gets on the other side of the transition

A **reusable AI Software Factory in commercial operation**: a governed system that turns an approved product idea into deployable software assembled from proven modules, with executive visibility (the Enterprise Catalog + Software Factory console), honest metrics, and human approval on everything that deploys, charges, or publishes.

## One-line recommendation

**Approve the transition: supply the commercial terms and authorize runtime ignition, and Herman Legacy moves from building the platform to producing and selling software from it — with the engineering foundation already proven complete.**
