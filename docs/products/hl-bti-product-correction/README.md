# HL-BTI — Product Correction

**Product Correction Order — product design only. No code was written, modified, or applied.** This redesigns the HL-BTI _product_ (not its architecture) around the CEO's vision: a consultant enters a business name and website, HL-BTI analyzes the business, finds opportunities, recommends Herman Legacy solutions, and produces an Executive Blueprint and a proposal — so the consultant can confidently ask for the customer's business.

## The correction in one paragraph

The product made a **scorecard** its face and made the **consultant** do the analysis by typing 43 scores. The CEO's product does the opposite: **the software analyzes; the consultant validates; evidence drives every conclusion; the output is understanding, a blueprint, and a proposal — not a score.** The good news: everything downstream of "findings" (transformation, recommendations, roadmap, blueprint, proposal) and everything upstream ("evidence store, collectors, website scanner") **already exists and is tested.** The one true gap is the **AI Business Analyst** that turns evidence into understanding — one new engine, built on the existing AI gateway. Add that, deploy, wire it, and re-face the app into one workflow, and HL-BTI becomes the product the CEO described and **passes the Success Test.**

**Reuse tally: ~11 reuse · 2 modify · 1 replace (manual scoring) · 1 new (AI analyst).** No duplicate platform service. Nothing has been implemented; this awaits CEO approval before controlled execution.
