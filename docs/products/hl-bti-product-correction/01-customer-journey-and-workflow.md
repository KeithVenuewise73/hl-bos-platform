# Corrected Customer Journey & Business Workflow (Deliverables 2 & 3)

**One seamless workflow.** No separate tools, dashboards, or modules — a single linear flow the consultant drives while sitting with the business owner.

## The customer journey (what the consultant + owner see)

### 1 · Start — "Analyze Business"

One screen. The consultant enters:

- Business Name · Website · Industry · Location

…and clicks **Analyze Business**. That is the entire input. (Everything the current app does — pick a client, open an engagement, open the assessment wizard — collapses into this one action.)

### 2 · Discovery (automatic, live)

HL-BTI runs discovery against the website and public signals and shows a live "analyzing…" state as evidence arrives:

- Website · SEO · Visibility · Technology · Performance · Reviews · Social · Business Listings · Security · Accessibility — from the automated collectors.
- Documents / Financials / Operations / Questionnaire answers — optional uploads the consultant can add, analyzed the same way.

The consultant does **not** score anything. HL-BTI collects the evidence.

### 3 · Business Intelligence Profile (understanding, not scores)

HL-BTI presents its **understanding** of the business: how it operates, its assets, technology, marketing, operations, customer experience, AI readiness, growth potential, business and operational risks, competitive advantages, and revenue/cost/automation/AI opportunities — each written in plain language and **linked to the evidence** behind it. This is the "we understand your business" moment that earns trust.

### 4 · Findings

HL-BTI presents the findings. Each shows: **Evidence · Business Impact · Confidence · Risk · Opportunity.** Nothing unsupported. The consultant reviews, adjusts wording where needed, and approves — the **validate** step, not a create step.

### 5 · Transformation opportunities

For every finding, HL-BTI has already determined _how the business can improve_, prioritized the improvements, and estimated impact, ROI (where evidence supports it), and implementation effort.

### 6 · Herman Legacy recommendations

For every opportunity, HL-BTI names the **Herman Legacy capability** that solves it — Herman Legacy Digital, VisibilityAI, HL-BOS CRM, HomeHuddle, Business Transformation Services, Marketing Services, AI Automation, Reputation Management, and future vertical operating systems. One opportunity may map to several. This is where analysis becomes revenue.

### 7 · Executive Business Transformation Blueprint

The professional deliverable, assembled automatically: Executive Summary · Business Intelligence Profile · Strengths · Weaknesses · Risks · Growth Opportunities · Transformation Opportunities · Recommended Herman Legacy Services · Quick Wins · 90-Day Roadmap · 12-Month Roadmap · Expected ROI · Implementation Timeline. Export/print ready.

### 8 · Proposal

Generated **directly from the blueprint** — scope, deliverables, recommended Herman Legacy services, timeline. The consultant reviews it with the owner and asks for the business.

## The business workflow (behind the one screen)

```
Consultant: Business Name + Website + Industry + Location  →  [Analyze Business]
        │
        ▼
Discovery: automated collectors gather evidence   →  Evidence Repository (single source of truth)
        │
        ▼
AI Analyst: reads the evidence  →  Business Intelligence Profile + evidence-backed Findings
        │   (every claim tied to evidence; confidence stated; no unsupported findings)
        ▼
Transformation: each finding → improvement, priority, impact, ROI, effort
        │
        ▼
Herman Legacy Recommendation: each opportunity → Herman Legacy product/service
        │
        ▼
Consultant Validation: review & approve (adjust wording, not originate data)
        │
        ▼
Executive Blueprint  →  Proposal  →  "Ask for the business"
```

## What the consultant does vs. what HL-BTI does

| HL-BTI does                                         | Consultant does                              |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Discover, collect evidence, understand the business | Enter name + website; add optional documents |
| Produce findings, opportunities, ROI, priorities    | **Review and approve** the findings          |
| Map opportunities to Herman Legacy services         | Confirm the recommended services fit         |
| Assemble the blueprint and the proposal             | Present them and ask for the business        |

The consultant is a **reviewer and closer** — never a data-entry clerk. That is the product.
