# HL-BTI — Corrected Product Definition (Deliverable 1)

**Product Correction Order · product design only · no code written, modified, or applied.**

## What HL-BTI is (one sentence)

**A consultant enters a business name and website; HL-BTI analyzes the business, finds the opportunities, maps them to Herman Legacy products and services, and produces a customer-ready Executive Business Transformation Blueprint and proposal — so the consultant can confidently ask for the customer's business.**

Everything else — schemas, engines, scores, dashboards — is implementation. The product is that one outcome.

## The product is the OUTPUT, not the tool

The current build made a **scorecard** the centerpiece. That is the wrong face for the product. The CEO's vision is explicit: **produce understanding, not scores.** The customer never came to see seven numbers between 0 and 100; they came to learn _what's holding their business back, what to do about it, and what it will cost._ So the product's primary artifacts are, in order:

1. A **Business Intelligence Profile** — HL-BTI's understanding of the business (how it operates, its assets, risks, and opportunities).
2. **Findings** — evidence, business impact, confidence, risk, opportunity. No unsupported findings.
3. **Recommendations** — each mapped to Herman Legacy products/services, each with why, expected impact, expected ROI, priority, difficulty, and implementation order.
4. **The Executive Business Transformation Blueprint** — the professional deliverable.
5. **A proposal** — generated directly from the blueprint.

Scores may exist internally as a summarizing device, but they are **not the product** and must not be the headline.

## The five non-negotiable rules, as product commitments

| Rule                                                                                 | What it means for the product                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **AI performs the analysis, not the consultant**                                     | The consultant enters name + website and clicks **Analyze**. HL-BTI does the discovery and analysis. The consultant is presented with completed analysis to review. |
| **Consultants validate, they don't create assessments**                              | The consultant's screen is a **review-and-approve** screen, never a data-entry form of 43 sliders.                                                                  |
| **Evidence drives conclusions**                                                      | Every finding shows the evidence it rests on. No evidence → no finding. Confidence is stated.                                                                       |
| **Every finding → one or more Herman Legacy solutions**                              | Findings are not academic; each names the Herman Legacy product/service that solves it.                                                                             |
| **Every recommendation explains why / impact / ROI / priority / difficulty / order** | The blueprint is decision-ready, not a list of observations.                                                                                                        |
| **One seamless workflow**                                                            | Not a suite of dashboards and modules — a single linear flow from "enter website" to "here is the proposal."                                                        |

## The honest reframing the CEO needs to hear

The hard truth from the last two reviews, stated plainly so the product target is real:

- **Today, HL-BTI does not analyze a business — a consultant types 43 scores and a deterministic engine formats them.** That is the exact opposite of Rule 1.
- **The consulting brain that produces findings/roadmap/recommendations is genuinely good — but it is fed opinions, not evidence, and it is invisible in the app.**
- The corrected product moves the work from the consultant to the software: **collect evidence → AI produces understanding + findings → consultant validates → blueprint + proposal.** That is the product. The rest of these documents define that experience and the shortest honest path to demonstrating it.
